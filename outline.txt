Skyborne Saga - Chapter 1: The Road to the Colosseum

The sea was calm when Kael stepped from the ferry onto the worn wooden docks of Paradise Island. Fishing boats bobbed gently against their moorings, and the salty breeze carried the scent of the ocean across the quiet village. To anyone else, it might have seemed like a peaceful place to settle down.

Kael wasn't looking for peace.

He was searching for meaning. 

Stories of Champions, Heroes, echoed across the world. There was no greater honor.

"Honor.. huh" Kael thought. 

Kael had been wandering taking odd jobs here and there to make ends meet. 

A Merc who must have taken one too many shots to the head, as he had no memory of where he was from anymore. 

All he could remember was how to fight, so he held on to what was left and set off to find his past.

He had yet to find someone who could tell him who he was.. but that was soon to change. 

"You alright there lad? you were just staring off into space for a while." a gruff voice called.

Kael snapped back to reality. "I'm good old man." 

The ferryman, Osk, leaned against a post with his arms crossed.

"If you're after work, talk to Mayor Orrin. He always has something needs doing."

Kael nodded and made his way into the village.

Mayor Orrin's expression brightened as soon as he saw the young swordsman headed his way.

"Need work do you?" he asked, almost in a giddy tone. "We've been waiting for a mercenary to show up, one of our own has gone missing in he Whispering Woods."

He folded his hands across his desk.

"Ranger Willa set off to investigate reports of monsters. She never returned."

"We have no fighters here, and everyone is too scared to venture into the woods with Willa gone." Orrin explains. 

Kael accepted the job without hesitation.

The Whispering Wood lived up to its name. Strange winds carried faint voices through the trees, and shadows shifted between ancient roots. Goblins had taken much of the forest as their territory, turning the trails into a maze of traps and ambushes.

Deep within their the forest, Kael finds a goblin encampment and faced Chieftain Skraag.

The goblin leader towered over his kin, draped in crude armor fashioned from scavenged metal and beast bones. His roar shook the camp as he charged.

The battle was fierce, but short.

Steel outmatched savagery.

When Skraag fell, the goblins scattered, and the thick thorn barrier blocking the northern trail withered away as if released from some ancient curse.

Beyond it stood the Old Oak Grove.

At its center waited Briarmaw.

Part tree, part ancient spirit, the guardian emerged from twisted roots and thorn-covered bark. Every swing of its massive limbs shattered stone and uprooted trees. Yet Kael pressed forward.

After a brutal struggle, Briarmaw finally collapsed.

Only then did Kael spot Ranger Willa who was finally able to free herself from Briarmaws roots.

Once freed, she revealed what she had been ambushed by the goblins who offering her up as a sacrifice to calm the spirits of the forest.

A few moments longer and she would have been devoured by the ancient spirit whole. 

Kael returned it to Orrin, who paid him well for the task.

Orrin lets Kael know that the village elder has asked to speak with him. 

Kael sought out Elder Mahina, the island's wise spiritual leader.

She listened patiently as he explained his plans.

"As fate would have it," Mahina said, smiling softly, "someone has been looking for an escort through Hollow Cave."

She pointed toward the stone arch near the cave entrance.

"There you'll find Sera."

Kael found a young healer waiting beneath the archway, carrying only a staff and a worn travel bag.

"You must be Kael," she said.

"And you must be the one seeking an escort."

Their partnership was simple. She needed protection. He needed to reach the Colosseum.

Together, they entered Hollow Cave.

The journey beneath the island was longer and stranger than either expected.

They passed abandoned smugglers' camps hidden within tunnels carved by forgotten hands.

They solved the Gallery's lamp puzzle, igniting ancient braziers until hidden passages revealed themselves.

They crossed a stone causeway stretching across an underground lake, where black waters disappeared into endless darkness.

Crystal caverns shimmered with unnatural light while abandoned mining tunnels echoed with ghostly sounds.

Deeper still, they reached a forgotten crypt guarded by three ancient seal-keepers.

One by one, the guardians fell.

At last a great stone door opened.

Beyond it lay the Sovereign's Hall.

Something was already fighting inside.

The clash of steel rang through the chamber.

Colosseum guards filled the hall, desperately battling a monstrous creature rising from a flooded throne.

The Drowned Sovereign.

Its bloated, ancient form towered above them, wrapped in rotting royal garments and dripping black water across the floor.

Captain Varro barked orders from the front lines while Guard Dren and the others struggled to keep the creature contained.

They were losing.

Badly.

One after another, guards were thrown aside.

The Sovereign's roar shook the hall.

Varro saw Kael and shouted, "Help us, damn it!"

Kael and Sera exchanged a glance.

Then they charged.

The battle pushed them to their limits.

Kael's sword carved through corrupted flesh while Sera's healing magic kept him standing when any other warrior would have fallen. Together they slowly chipped away at the ancient horror.

Finally, with one last strike, Kael drove his blade into the Sovereign's heart.

The monster released an earth-shaking scream before collapsing into the dark waters below.

The hall fell silent.

Victory.

But it came at a cost.

The moment the battle ended, exhaustion overtook Kael. His wounds were too severe.

His vision blurred.

Then everything went black.

When Kael awoke, he wasn't greeted as a hero.

He woke to iron bars.

Chains.

The stench of sweat and despair.

He sat upright, realizing he was locked inside a prison cell.

Nearby, a harsh-looking man watched him through the bars.

"About time you woke up," he said.

"Who are you?" Kael demanded.

"Overseer Vex."

The answer came with a cruel smile.

Vex leaned closer.

"Varro's men brought you in. Couldn't return empty-handed after all the soldiers they lost. So they claimed you as their prize."

The words hit harder than any sword.

"You mean I'm a prisoner?"

"A slave."

Silence hung between them.

Only one thought mattered.

"Where is Sera?"

Vex shrugged.

"The healer? She's alive. They sent her to the Colosseum infirmary."

Relief washed over Kael, but only for a moment.

Beyond the bars he could hear distant cheers.

The roar of crowds.

The thunder of battle.

The Colosseum.

He had finally reached his destination.

Not as a challenger.

Not as a champion.

But as property.

Vex laughed as he walked away.

"If you want freedom, swordsman, you'll have to earn it."

Kael tightened his fists around the cold iron bars and listened to the crowd roar somewhere above.

The real battle had only just begun.

THE SHATTERED GOD MYTHOS (Protagonist + Antagonist Interconnection)
1. The Domain Before the Multiverse
There was once one being — a god‑like, reality‑defining entity known as the Primarch.

The Primarch didn’t rule the domain like a tyrant.
They were the domain.

Every law of physics was their will

Every realm was an extension of their consciousness

Every soul existed within their dream

They were not benevolent or malevolent — they were simply absolute.

⚔️ 2. The Fall — The Shattering
A rebellion, a cosmic betrayal, or a catastrophic event (you decide the flavor) defeated the Primarch.

But killing a being made of pure existence is impossible.

So instead, their being fractured.

Their consciousness exploded outward, splintering into countless shards — each shard becoming:

a new dimension

a new timeline

a new physical law

a new “version” of reality

The multiverse is literally the corpse of the Primarch.

Every universe is a piece of them.

🧩 3. The Protagonist — The Lost Core Fragment
Your protagonist is one of the largest, most important fragments of the Primarch’s original being.

But they don’t know that.

When the Primarch shattered, this fragment was flung into a pod, sealed, and buried deep within the Nexus.
Over time, it developed:

its own identity

its own memories

its own emotions

its own sense of self

They are a person, not a god.

But they are also a piece of the original god.

Their amnesia isn’t random — it’s the result of being forcibly separated from the whole.

👁️ 4. The Antagonist — The Original Consciousness
The Primarch’s core consciousness survived the shattering.

But it is incomplete.

It is:

unstable

fragmented

enraged

desperate

half‑formed

missing essential pieces of identity

This core consciousness has been traveling the multiverse, dimension by dimension, assimilating fragments of itself.

Every time it absorbs a shard:

it grows stronger

it becomes more coherent

it remembers more

it becomes more dangerous

It is rebuilding itself.

And the protagonist is the final, largest missing piece.

🕳️ 5. Their Interconnection
The protagonist and antagonist are not just connected —
they are the same being, separated by cosmic trauma.

The antagonist sees the protagonist as:

stolen identity

stolen power

stolen memories

stolen purpose

The protagonist sees the antagonist as:

a monster

a threat

a cosmic force

a destiny they refuse to accept

But the truth is:
They are two halves of the same god.

🔥 6. The Antagonist’s Goal
Reclaim the final fragment.
Reassemble the Primarch.
Restore the original domain.
Erase the multiverse (because it is “wrong,” a broken state).
Return everything to the singular, perfect existence that existed before.

They believe this is justice, not evil.

🌑 7. The Protagonist’s Crisis
As the antagonist grows stronger, the protagonist begins to experience:

visions of the original domain

memories that aren’t theirs

emotions that feel alien

power surges they can’t control

whispers of the Primarch’s original will

glimpses of what they used to be

They must decide:

Do they remain who they’ve become?
Or do they accept the truth and risk becoming the Primarch again?

⚡ 8. The Final Conflict
The antagonist doesn’t want to kill the protagonist.

They want to merge with them.

But merging means:

the protagonist’s identity dissolves

their memories vanish

their personality is overwritten

the Primarch returns

the multiverse collapses

The protagonist must fight the original version of themselves to preserve the existence of every universe.
